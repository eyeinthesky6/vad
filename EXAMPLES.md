# VAD Examples

This repository includes four preliminary example packages.

## 1. Simple invoice

Demonstrates:

- issuer
- recipient
- amount
- due date
- payment instruction
- financial-action restrictions

Path: `examples/simple-invoice/`

## 2. Contract clause

Demonstrates:

- parties
- clause text
- obligations
- deadlines
- human-review rules for legal interpretation

Path: `examples/contract-clause/`

## 3. Satsang transcript

Demonstrates:

- source audio
- speaker
- transcript content
- glossary terms
- human approval state

Path: `examples/satsang-transcript/`

## 4. Tender brief

Demonstrates:

- tender deadline
- EMD
- eligibility
- required documents
- action restrictions

Path: `examples/tender-brief/`
